FAQs

Common questions.

What's the difference between hire purchase and leasing?

With hire purchase, you typically own the asset once all payments are made. With leasing, you pay to use the asset over a set period without ever taking ownership.

Can I refinance equipment I already own?

Yes — this is a common way to release capital tied up in vehicles, machinery, or equipment, using the asset itself as security rather than borrowing against property.

What types of assets can be financed?

Vehicles, plant and construction equipment, manufacturing machinery, medical and office equipment, and many other specialist asset types, depending on lender appetite.

Does the asset need to be new?

No — both new and used assets can typically be financed, though terms and lender choice may vary depending on the asset's age and condition.
